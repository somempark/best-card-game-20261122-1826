**Emberfall** — an original duel card game, built from scratch in vanilla JavaScript on a 2D canvas. No libraries, no network requests, no copied assets: one original game of ember, tide, thorn and storm.

You and a rival each hold 20 life and a 30-card deck of sixteen original cards in four elements — Ember, Tide, Thorn and Storm, each told apart by colour and shape. Every turn your ember refills and you draw a card; play as many cards as your ember allows, then hand the table over. Chain your elements: a second card of one element in a turn gains +2 Resonance, and a card costlier than the last gains +1 Rising Chain. The rival shows the card it plans to open with, so you can read it and answer — guard the big hits, race a slow rival, and strike for lethal.

## What's in the game

- **A complete duel** — title screen, table with your hand and the rival's, readable cards with cost and power, life and shield bars, ember crystals, and win and loss screens with your score.
- **Sixteen original cards, four elements** — strikes, guards, surges and drains, each with its own drawn glyph (flame, droplet, leaf, bolt) in its own colour.
- **Real decisions every hand** — Resonance and Rising Chain combos, a readable rival AI with a visible intent banner, and a shield that only lasts until your next turn.
- **Feel and polish** — cards fly from hand to arena, damage numbers float, the table shakes on a hit, and a win lands with confetti.
- **Synthesized sound** — card snaps, hits, guard chimes and a victory fanfare, all made live with WebAudio. **M** mutes.
- **Keyboard and mouse** — click a card to play it, **1–5** play from the keyboard, arrows move the selection, **Space** ends the turn, **Enter** starts and plays again.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Keyboard and mouse required.
